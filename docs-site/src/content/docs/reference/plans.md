## Upgrading and downgrading

You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel at any time from the [billing dashboard](https://app.confire.dev/dashboard/billing).

- **Upgrades** take effect immediately. You're charged a prorated amount for the remainder of the current period.
- **Downgrades** take effect at the end of the current billing period.
- **Cancellations** take effect at the end of the current billing period — you keep access until then.

Annual plans are non-refundable after 14 days. If you cancel within 14 days of your first annual payment, contact [hello@confire.dev](mailto:hello@confire.dev) for a refund.
